There is vast parallel construction going on in Jerusalem. There is construction for the light
rail projects and their expansion. There is unfettered high-rise construction. It is all
happening all at once.

The most basic premises of decent planning seem to have gone completely out of the window.

My wife and I have a thirteen-month-old son. You have main thoroughfares that literally just
end. There is no signage. There is no centralised mechanism of notification.

That last point is the one I keep coming back to. It is not the disruption itself — a city that
is building a light rail network is going to be disrupted, and I would rather it were built than
not. It is that there is nowhere to find out. No single place that says which road is closed
this week, for how long, and what the alternative is. You discover it by walking into it, and
with a pushchair you discover it late.
