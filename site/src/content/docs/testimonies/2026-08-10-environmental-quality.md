What actually improves the daily experience of living in this city is, to a good extent, the
quality of the environment. Not the arguments the city is loudest about — see
[the argument the city is having](../context/the-ideological-agenda.md) — but the ambient
conditions you live inside every day.

Two of them, as a separate point.

There is insane honking in Jerusalem. Drivers here honk their horns unnecessarily. It is a
city-wide problem.

And air quality is significantly lower than it should be for a Western capital.
