Countless traders in the city centre are reporting that their businesses are shuttering. They
cannot afford to keep them open. There is no compensation offered by the city — no meaningful
compensation. So you have small businesses closing left, right and centre.

## The asymmetry

There is not only a lack of proactive information to residents; there is a lack of — I would even
say — compensation.

I do not know whether there is any precedent for a municipality reducing the arnona it charges.
But this is a system, a municipality, that knows how to take and gives very reluctantly.
Collection enforcement is extremely rigorous.

That asymmetry is the part I would want tested. The same institution that will pursue an unpaid
bill with real efficiency does not appear to have any equivalent machinery pointing the other way.
