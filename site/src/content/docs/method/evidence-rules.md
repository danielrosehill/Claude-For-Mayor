These rules apply at every stage of [the pipeline](../pipeline.md). They exist because a
platform that claims to be evidence-based is only worth its weakest claim — and the
characteristic failure mode of AI-assisted research is a confident number with no source behind
it.

- **No invented numbers.** A statistic without a source is marked
  `[UNVERIFIED — verify before publication]` and blocks stage advancement.

- **Preferred sources**, in order:
  1. The municipality's own published record — approved budget, council and committee protocols,
     bylaws (חוקי עזר), tenders, plans, and the municipal auditor (מבקר העירייה).
  2. CBS (הלשכה המרכזית לסטטיסטיקה) — the local authorities file and neighbourhood-level
     statistical areas.
  3. Jerusalem Institute for Policy Research (מכון ירושלים למחקרי מדיניות) — the Statistical
     Yearbook of Jerusalem and *Jerusalem: Facts and Trends*.
  4. State Comptroller (מבקר המדינה), including the local-government audit.
  5. Ministry of the Interior (משרד הפנים).
  6. Knesset Research and Information Center (מרכז המחקר והמידע של הכנסת).
  7. Bank of Israel, OECD, peer-reviewed research.
  8. Quality journalism — last resort, always flagged. A council row that made the local press is
     reported, not proven; find the protocol.

- **Instruments are cited precisely**, and municipal work runs mostly on *subordinate*
  instruments. A bylaw, ministerial circular, licensing condition, tender clause or budget line
  gets the same treatment as a statute: official name in Hebrew and English, the issuing
  authority, the date, and the specific clause. Verify against the actual text; do not cite
  provisions from memory.

- **No power without a citation.** "The municipality is responsible for X" is a claim like any
  other and needs the provision that says so. See [the powers doctrine](./powers-doctrine.md);
  this is the rule the whole project turns on.

- **No promise without a mechanism, and no mechanism without a funding source.** Every costed
  recommendation names the budget line and says what gets less. "Efficiency savings" is not a
  source. Anything relying on arnona (ארנונה, municipal property tax) income must establish the
  current rule on raising it and address the approval risk.

- **Testimony is evidence of experience, not prevalence.** Prevalence claims require data. At
  city scale this bites harder than nationally: one neighbourhood's problem can be entirely real
  and entirely unrepresentative. Say which neighbourhoods.

- **Disaggregate.** City-wide averages hide the thing municipal policy exists to address. Report
  by neighbourhood or statistical area wherever the source allows, and where a source does not
  cover part of the city, record that — missing data about a neighbourhood is a finding, and
  often the most important one.

- **Adopted is not implemented, and implemented is not funded.** State which, with the date
  checked.

- **Trade-offs mandatory.** Every proposal names its losers — including the neighbourhood that
  loses and the department that absorbs the work — and answers the strongest objection, not the
  weakest.
