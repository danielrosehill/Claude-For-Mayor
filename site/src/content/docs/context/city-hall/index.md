## The council as elected, February 2024

31 seats; 16 for a majority.

| Bloc | Lists | Seats |
|---|---|:---:|
| **Haredi** | Degel HaTorah (6), Shas (6), Agudat Yisrael (3), Bnei Torah (1) | **16** |
| **Religious-Zionist / right** | United (2), Religious Zionist Party (2), Likud (1), Noam (1) | **6** |
| **Mayor's list** | Our Jerusalem | **2** |
| **Opposition / secular-pluralist** | Jerusalem Union (4), Hitorerut (3) | **7** |

Two features of this arithmetic drive everything downstream:

- **The Haredi lists hold a bare majority on their own** — 16 of 31. Any proposal that needs a
  council vote needs either their support or a coalition assembled around them, and there is no
  majority that excludes them.
- **The mayor holds two seats.** Moshe Lion won the mayoralty with 81% of the vote and commands
  2/31 of the chamber. Mayoral authority here is coalition authority, and a platform that assumes
  a directly elected mayor can simply direct the city has misread the office.

This is why stage 4 of [the pipeline](../../pipeline.md) includes an explicit council-arithmetic
check, and why a proposal's class under [the powers doctrine](../../method/powers-doctrine.md) is
necessary but not sufficient: a class-A power still needs the votes.

## What needs researching

The research plan for this section, in priority order:

1. **The coalition as actually constituted** — which lists signed, on what terms, and whether the
   coalition agreements are published. Coalition agreements are where a commitment gets traded
   away, and they are the highest-value document in this whole section.
2. **Portfolio allocation** — deputy mayors, committee chairs, and in particular who chairs
   finance and the local planning and building committee. Committee chairs decide what reaches
   the chamber.
3. **Any changes since February 2024** — resignations, splits, coalition departures, by-election
   or replacement councillors. Two and a half years is long enough for the 2024 table to be
   wrong.
4. **The administration's record** — the budget as approved each year and as executed, the
   municipal auditor's reports, State Comptroller findings on the municipality, and the
   multi-year plans published and their delivery rate.
5. **Faction profiles** — one per list, covering its base, its published positions, and the
   motions its members have actually moved. These feed the
   [comparison matrices](../matrices/index.md).

Every item here is a research task under the same evidence rules as the policy work: Hebrew
primary sources, protocols over press reports, and a date on every claim.
