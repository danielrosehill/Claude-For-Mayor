The honest state of the project, kept in public. Verification debt is listed here rather than
quietly carried, because a platform that argues from sourced evidence does not get to hide its
unsourced parts.

## Where it stands

The **method is built and the content has just started**. The pipeline, the plugin, the powers
doctrine and the context scaffold exist. On 10 August 2026 the first testimony landed — a single
dictated account that opened three areas at once. All three sit at stage 1, and under our own
rules nothing in them may be proposed until the jurisdiction gate has been run.

## Verification debt

Claims currently resting on secondary sources or on nothing, in priority order. Each blocks
something.

| # | Claim | Current basis | Needed | Blocks |
|:-:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The arnona rule — that rates cannot be raised without joint Interior and Finance approval | Working assumption, uncited | The current statutory provision, the approval criteria, and Jerusalem's record of seeking deviations | Every costed proposal, and now the **renting** area directly |
| 2 | The bylaw-making process and the Interior Minister's confirmation requirement | Municipalities Ordinance, chapter identified but not read | The operative sections, the confirmation criteria, typical elapsed time, Jerusalem's recent record | Every class-B proposal |
| 3 | The 2024 election results table | English Wikipedia, accessed 2026-08-10 | Ministry of the Interior published results | Council arithmetic in stage 4 |
| 4 | The next election date | Inferred from the five-year cycle | The Local Authorities (Elections) Law, and whether the 2024 postponements moved the cycle | Nothing structural — the project is not timed to it |
| 5 | East Jerusalem franchise and turnout | Commonly repeated figures, not verified | A primary or reputable statistical source | Any city-wide prevalence claim |
| 6 | Community administrations (מנהלים קהילתיים) — formal status and powers | Named only | Their statutory or contractual basis, budget and relationship to the municipality | Any proposal routed through them |
| 7 | The balancing grant and Jerusalem-specific funding | Named only | Amounts, formula, conditionality | Fiscal envelope in stage 4 |
| 8 | Who is statutorily liable for arnona — the *mahzik* (מחזיק) rule — and whether the municipality has any discretion over whose name a bill sits in | Testimony only | The operative sections of the Municipalities Ordinance, and Jerusalem's registration practice | The whole **renting** area |
| 9 | Jerusalem's arnona is the highest in the country | Testimony only | A like-for-like comparison on a stated measure, across a stated comparator set, for a stated year | The renting problem statement |
| 10 | Jerusalem is consistently the poorest city in Israel | Testimony only | CBS socio-economic index and poverty measures; the ranking differs by measure and the measure has to be named | The **housing-supply** problem statement |
| 11 | Which bodies actually deliver the light rail works, and the municipality's formal role | Nothing | The delivering entities, their ownership, and the permit relationship with the city | The **construction-disruption** jurisdiction map — and probably its headline finding |

## Next actions

**Blocking everything:**

1. **Run `/map-powers` on the three open areas.** Nothing may be researched, drafted or proposed
   in any of them until the jurisdiction gate has been cleared. Suggested order:
   **construction-disruption** first, because its attributions are the most likely to be wrong
   and it is therefore the sharpest test of the skill; then **renting**, which has the clearest
   candidate for an unused class-A power; then **housing-supply**, which depends most on the
   standing powers primer being done.

**Runnable now, independent of the threads:**

2. **Build the verified powers primer** — items 1, 2, 6 and 7 above. This is the largest piece of
   standing research in the project and every policy area will draw on it, so doing it once
   properly beats doing it six times partially.
3. **Research the City Hall baseline** — the coalition as constituted, portfolio and committee
   allocation, any changes since February 2024, and the administration's record. Coalition
   agreements first; they are the highest-value document in that section.
4. **Re-verify the 2024 results** against the Ministry of the Interior, and settle the next
   election date.

**Deferred:**

5. Cover image and Open Graph image for the site — the landing page and social cards currently
   have neither.
6. First run of `/map-powers` will be the plugin's real test. Expect the command and the skill to
   need revision afterwards; that revision is part of the experiment, not a defect.
7. Broaden the testimony base. Three areas resting on one household is the project's biggest
   methodological weakness right now, and no amount of stage-2 research fully fixes it.

## Candidate threads

Not commitments, and not chosen — these are areas where a Jerusalem resident plausibly has a
first-person account, listed so the blank page is less blank. The user picks; an area only opens
when there is testimony behind it.

Cleanliness and refuse collection · street and pavement condition · parking and enforcement ·
the light rail and bus network as experienced rather than as planned · building permits and the
local planning committee · construction noise, dust and the permit conditions nobody can look up ·
parks, playgrounds and shade · the community administrations · what the municipality publishes and
what it does not · services in neighbourhoods that get less of them · animal control · signage and
wayfinding · what happens when you call the municipal hotline · **Jerusalem as an economic and
professional constraint** — fewer jobs, lower pay, a cost of living close to the centre's; raised
in the [opening account](./context/whose-account.md) but not yet opened, because it is not yet
clear the municipality holds a lever over it.

The ones worth doing are the ones where the resident's assumption about who is responsible turns
out to be wrong, or where a class-A power has been sitting unused. Neither is knowable in advance
of the testimony.
