This folder holds the **meta-proposal** — the rolled-up program for the city, synthesized from
the completed policy papers in [policy areas](../policy/index.md).

**It is empty, and will stay empty until the first policy area passes stage 3** of the
[pipeline](../pipeline.md): a compiled paper with sourced evidence, class-tagged and costed
mechanisms, and a steelmanned trade-offs section. The program is the distillation, not the
workspace.

`program-for-the-city.md` will be created by `/update-platform` once the first paper lands.

## How it will be organised

Not by theme. **By deliverability**, because that is the first honest question about any mayoral
platform and the document should answer it before it argues anything. Three tables come before
the policy sections:

1. **What the council can do on its own motion** — every class-A recommendation across every
   area, with its cost and the department that would carry it.
2. **What needs an approval, and from whom** — class B, with the approving body named.
3. **What needs a partner, or is advocacy only** — class C and D, with the counterpart named and
   the mayor's actual leverage stated rather than assumed.

The classes come from [the powers doctrine](../method/powers-doctrine.md). A platform whose
class-A table is short and whose class-D table is long is telling the truth about the office, and
padding the first table by reclassifying is exactly the failure the doctrine exists to prevent.

Three further checks run before anything is published:

- **Department capacity.** Municipal delivery capacity is more binding than money. Four proposals
  landing on the same understaffed unit is a conflict even if each is individually funded.
- **Fiscal coherence.** A running total, stated as a share of the relevant budget lines rather
  than in absolute shekels alone.
- **Council arithmetic.** What majority each recommendation needs, and whether any plausible
  coalition on [the council as it stands](../context/city-hall/index.md) delivers it. A program
  that cannot pass its own council is a finding worth publishing, not a reason to soften the
  program.

Conflicts that survive all of that go in an **open tensions** section at the end, rather than
being papered over.
