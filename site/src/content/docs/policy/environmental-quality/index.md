Opened by [The horns, and the air](../../testimonies/2026-08-10-environmental-quality.md),
10 August 2026.

**The thesis, provisionally.** The account's larger argument is that what actually changes daily
life here is ambient quality rather than the arguments the city is loudest about — the framing is
set out in [the argument the city is having](../../context/the-ideological-agenda.md). This area
holds the two ambient conditions named: horn noise and air quality.

It is also the area where the [powers doctrine](../../method/powers-doctrine.md) is most likely to
produce an uncomfortable answer. Unnecessary horn use is a traffic offence, and traffic offences
are enforced by Israel Police, not by City Hall. Air quality standards are national. If both come
back **C** or **D**, that is a finding worth publishing rather than a reason to drop the area —
but it does mean this is the thinnest of the seven areas on evidence and the most likely to end as
advocacy with a named counterpart.

The testimony behind it is two sentences long. That is stated here rather than disguised: it is
enough to open an area and nowhere near enough to propose anything.

Nothing below stage 1 has been done.

## Scope notes

### Facts to establish

| # | Claim or question | Status |
|:-:|---|---|
| 1 | Measured air quality in Jerusalem — PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, O₃ — by monitoring station and by year, from the national monitoring network, against both Israeli standards and WHO guideline values | `[UNVERIFIED]` |
| 2 | "Significantly lower than it should be for a Western capital" — a comparative claim needing a stated comparator set, a stated measure and a stated year. It also needs the regional dust baseline controlled for, or the comparison dropped | `[UNVERIFIED]` |
| 3 | Dominant local sources of the measured pollution: traffic, domestic heating, construction dust, regional dust events. Construction dust would connect this area to [construction noise](../construction-noise/index.md) and [disruption](../construction-disruption/index.md) | `[UNVERIFIED]` |
| 4 | Municipal duties and powers under the Clean Air Law (חוק אוויר נקי, 2008) — whether a local authority must produce an air quality plan, and what it may do about vehicle emissions in its own area | `[UNVERIFIED]` |
| 5 | Whether a low-emission zone is available to an Israeli municipality, on what instrument, and whether any has established one | `[UNVERIFIED]` |
| 6 | Levers the city plainly holds regardless: its own fleet, bus and refuse-vehicle procurement, dust-suppression conditions on construction sites, and traffic management | `[UNVERIFIED]` |
| 7 | The legal position on unnecessary horn use — the operative Traffic Regulations provision, the penalty, and which body enforces it | `[UNVERIFIED]` |
| 8 | Whether any enforcement data on horn use exists for Jerusalem, in any quantity | `[UNVERIFIED]` |
| 9 | Whether any city anywhere has measurably reduced horn use, by what instrument, and with what evidence — Mumbai, Karachi and Paris are the commonly cited attempts and at least one is an instructive failure | `[UNVERIFIED]` |
| 10 | Whether the honking is city-wide or concentrated, and where. A one-household observation cannot carry a city-wide claim under [the neighbourhood discipline](../../method/evidence-rules.md) | `[UNVERIFIED]` |

### Attributions to test

| # | The account assumes | To test |
|:-:|---|---|
| 1 | Horn use is the municipality's problem to solve | Traffic enforcement is Israel Police. Expect **D** on enforcement — the strongest wrong-attribution candidate in the project after the light rail. Whatever municipal lever exists is in road design, signage, junction layout and campaigning, and it should be named specifically or conceded |
| 2 | Air quality is the municipality's to fix | Standards and monitoring are national. Expect **C**, with the municipal levers itemised — fleet, planning conditions, site dust control, traffic management — rather than gestured at |
| 3 | A Western capital is the right benchmark | The benchmark is itself contestable given the regional dust baseline. If it cannot be controlled for, the honest move is to benchmark against Israeli cities instead and say why |
| 4 | Ambient environmental quality is what most improves daily life | The account's central framing claim, and a testable one — resident satisfaction surveys and the municipality's own service data exist. If it is wrong, a lot of this platform's emphasis is wrong |
