**Eight areas open**, all opened on 10 August 2026 by a single dictated account given in three
sittings. All eight sit at stage 1: testimony exists, and nothing has been verified. No
jurisdiction map has been built yet, which under
[the powers doctrine](../method/powers-doctrine.md) means nothing may be proposed.

An area opens when there is a first-person account of the problem to open it with — not when
someone decides the topic sounds important. It advances when the evidence does.

## How an area opens

A policy area starts with a [testimony](../testimonies/index.md): a dated, first-person account
of the thing going wrong, dictated and then cleaned by `/capture-testimony`. Everything else
follows from it.

From there each area moves through [the same four stages](../pipeline.md) and is held to
[the same evidence rules](../method/evidence-rules.md), with the extra gate that has no national
equivalent — [the powers doctrine](../method/powers-doctrine.md), which requires every proposal
to be traced to a named instrument and a named holder of it before it can be published.

None of them advances because a deadline arrived.
