Opened by [A poor city building luxury towers](../../testimonies/2026-08-10-housing-supply.md),
10 August 2026.

**The thesis, provisionally.** The stock being added to Jerusalem is not addressed to the income
profile of the people living in it. If that is true, the question this area exists to answer is
how much of it the municipality could have done differently — unit mix, affordable-housing
requirements, and the treatment of dwellings held as investments rather than lived in — and how
much sits with the district committee or with national policy.

Nothing below stage 1 has been done. Note also that this testimony is **observation rather than
direct experience**, which the evidence rules treat as weaker; it will need the data annex to
carry it much earlier than a typical area would.

### Attributions to test

| # | The account assumes | To test |
|:-:|---|---|
| 1 | "The municipality is fixated on building" this stock — i.e. that the city chooses what goes up | The split between the local planning committee (ועדה מקומית), the district committee (ועדה מחוזית) and national outline plans. Expect a **mixed A/B/C** answer, and expect the mix itself to be the finding |
| 2 | The city could require smaller or affordable units in what it approves | Turns on whether an affordable-housing quota instrument exists and is available at municipal level. If it exists and is unused, that is a class-**A** finding of the kind the doctrine hunts for |
| 3 | Marketing to diaspora buyers is something the city could restrain | Almost certainly not a municipal power over marketing as such. Expect **D** — but test whether it can be reached indirectly, e.g. through taxation of unoccupied dwellings |
| 4 | Investment ownership and empty flats are a municipal matter | Depends entirely on fact 7. **A, B or D** — one of the sharper open questions in this area |
| 5 | That building luxury high-rise and having a housing crisis are causally linked | Not a jurisdiction question but a substantive one, and the strongest objection to the whole area: added supply at any price band may still relieve pressure. It gets steelmanned in the paper, not waved away |
